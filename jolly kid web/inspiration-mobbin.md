# Jolly Kids Montessori — Inspiration Board (from Mobbin)

> Curated from Mobbin's **Sites** collection (marketing-website designs), browsed 2026-09-20. Mobbin has no Montessori/education-institute sites, so these are the **best modern patterns** pulled from top sites — to be re-skinned into Jolly Kids' warm, credible, education aesthetic (see `design-research.md` for palette/type). Each entry says what to *steal*, not copy.

---

## How to use this
Mobbin organizes marketing sites by **section pattern** (Hero, Stats, Social Proof, Features, How It Works, About, Footer, Pricing, FAQ) and **style** (Photography, Motion, Minimal, Dark, Colorful). The redesign should assemble Jolly Kids from these proven section patterns. Below are the standout references per section.

---

## 1. HERO patterns

### ⭐ Acctual — "tactile scattered objects" hero (top pick for Jolly Kids)
- **What it is:** bold headline center, with real physical objects (keyboard, paperclip, receipts) photographed and scattered around the edges on a warm/light background. Small trust pill above the headline ("Used by 5,000+ businesses worldwide").
- **Steal for Jolly Kids:** replace the office objects with **Montessori materials** — pink tower, golden beads, wooden sandpaper letters, colored knobbed cylinders, geometric solids. Warm cream background. Headline like *"Become a certified Montessori teacher."* Trust pill: *"1000+ teachers trained · 10+ countries."* This is authentic, ownable, and instantly says "Montessori" without being childish.

### ⭐ Titan — "headline + inline credibility stats" hero
- **What it is:** big confident headline ("Wealth managed from the palm of your hand"), two buttons, and a **row of 3 hard stats pinned to the bottom of the hero** ($1.1B AUM · 0.4% fee · 10 Awards Won) + a tasteful illustration on the right.
- **Steal for Jolly Kids:** end the hero with an inline stat strip — **Since 2019 · 1000+ Teachers · 10+ Countries · 7 Awards.** Instant credibility above the fold. Pair with a warm photo of the founder/classroom on the right instead of the engraving.

### Biograph — full-bleed photographic emotional hero
- **What it is:** edge-to-edge atmospheric photo, short emotive headline overlaid bottom-left ("Act now, live longer"), minimal nav.
- **Steal for Jolly Kids:** an alternate hero using a **real, warm classroom/teaching photo** full-bleed with an emotional line ("Shape little minds. Build a lifelong career."). Great if the client provides strong photography/video. Keep text legible with a soft gradient scrim.

## 2. STATS patterns (Jolly Kids has great numbers — lean in)

### ⭐ Norma — clean 4-column stat row
- **What it is:** a simple horizontal band of 4 big numbers with small labels underneath (220 phone checks · 150 unlocks · 23 minutes · 4h screen time), plus a one-line closer and a button.
- **Steal for Jolly Kids:** the cleanest, most reusable stat band. Use for **Since 2019 · 1000+ Teachers Trained · 10+ Countries · 8 Courses** (or add 7 Awards). Put it right under the hero or between sections.

### ⭐ Ramp — "stats-as-bento + customer proof" grid
- **What it is:** headline "Healthier businesses run on Ramp," then a **bento grid of cards, each pairing a big stat with a real customer logo** (18 days saved · 15 hrs/week · 4 tools replaced · 100% · $100M).
- **Steal for Jolly Kids:** a bento mixing stats with **social proof** — e.g. cards like *"350+ Montessori materials," "44 phonic sounds taught," "10+ countries placed,"* each tied to a graduate photo/quote or a partner-school logo. Combines numbers + trust in one section.

## 3. SOCIAL PROOF patterns

### ⭐ ClickUp — "Trusted by the best" logo strip
- **What it is:** a slim horizontal band under the hero: *"TRUSTED BY THE BEST"* + a row of recognizable logos (Wayfair, Deloitte, Pfizer, Adobe, NBCUniversal).
- **Steal for Jolly Kids:** two versions —
  1. **Accreditation strip:** *"Accredited & Recognized"* + logos for **World Skill Council (London, UK), Central Govt. Nodal Agency, AMI, IMTC, Jolly Phonics UK.** This is Jolly Kids' single biggest trust lever — put it high on the homepage.
  2. **Placement strip:** *"Our graduates teach at"* + logos of CBSE / Matriculation / International / Montessori schools where alumni work.

### Testimonials
- Old site has 3 real testimonials (Shajoth Kumar, Mary Charlet Jasmine, Vidhya Ganapathy) — build a **testimonial wall/carousel** with photo + name + location + course-year. Mobbin's masonry testimonial patterns (avatar + quote card) fit well. Emphasize the **global** angle (Sharjah/UAE alumni) and career outcomes.

## 4. Other sections to assemble (patterns to look up on Mobbin)
- **How It Works** — a 3–4 step path: *Enquire → Free Consultation → Enroll → Get Certified & Placed.* (Mobbin "How It Works" section filter.)
- **Features** — the 4 value pillars (Expert Faculty, Holistic Approach, Practical Experience, Community) as an icon-card grid.
- **Pricing/Courses** — course cards with duration badges (1 yr / 6 mo / 3 mo) and a "Request fees" CTA. (Mobbin "Pricing" patterns adapt well to a course catalog.)
- **FAQ** — eligibility, batches/timings, medium (Tamil/English), certification validity, fees.
- **Footer** — contact, map, social (Facebook/Instagram), quick links, accreditation badges.

## 5. Style direction confirmed by the research
- **Warm + credible**, not cartoonish (audience = adult career-changers). Cream backgrounds, navy text, amber CTAs (matches WSC certificate colors).
- **Real photography/video** is the biggest quality lever — the strongest reference sites (Biograph, Acctual, Ramp) all win on authentic imagery. → Prioritize getting real photos from the client.
- **Rounded, friendly type + generous whitespace** (Poppins/Quicksand headings, Inter/Nunito body — see `design-research.md`).
- **Two clear entry paths** on the homepage: *For Aspiring Teachers* (courses) and *For Schools* (B2B services).
