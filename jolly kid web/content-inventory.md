## 0. Analysis Summary

**What the business is:** A Montessori teacher-training institute in Nagercoil, Tamil Nadu, India, founded 2019. It trains adults (housewives, working professionals, graduates) to become certified Montessori / early-childhood teachers, with Central Government certification valid in India and abroad. There is also a sister Montessori *school* for children.

**Site pages (7):** Home, About Us, Courses, Gallery (184 photos), Testimonials, Terms & Conditions, Contact.

**Primary goal of the site:** Lead generation — get prospective students to "Book a Free Consultation" and enquire about the courses.

### Problems with the old site (redesign priorities)
1. **Spam / hacked content** — the homepage contains injected German online-gambling casino spam text and an outbound link. **Do NOT carry this over.** It signals the old site was compromised — the redesign should be a clean rebuild.
2. **Broken stat counters** — About page shows "0 Courses / 0+ Happy Students / 0% Success %" (counter animation never populated). Needs real numbers.
3. **Unstyled / dated theme** — heavy page-builder shortcodes, no modern layout, weak mobile experience, generic look.
4. **Weak hierarchy & CTAs** — key selling points (Govt. certification, placement, own-school) are buried in carousels.
5. **Testimonials page is empty** without JS; only 3 testimonials exist.
6. **Placeholder testimonial copy** — the "Leave your testimonial" block wrongly says "start your **accounting** career" (copy-paste error to fix).

### Recommended new information architecture
- **Home** (hero + value props + courses preview + why-choose + testimonials + awards + CTA)
- **About** (founder story + specialities + stats + gallery preview)
- **Courses** (overview + 5 course cards → detail, curriculum, eligibility, career prospects, certification benefits)
- **Gallery**
- **Testimonials**
- **Contact** (form + map + details)
- Footer with quick links, contact, social.

### Diploma course details
- The Diploma Course covers the **3 to 6 age range**; the Elementary Diploma Course covers the **6 to 12 age range**. The Primary Montessori Diploma qualifies the holder to apply the Montessori method to children aged **2½–6 years** (theory + "learning by doing" practical classes).
- **Duration / batches:** Two batches conducted annually — First session **June–December**, Second session **December–May**. Monday–Friday, **9:30 am – 1:00 pm**. Application forms available from June (first session) and December (second session).
- **Eligibility:** Above 18 years / 10th Pass / 12th Pass / Graduates / Equivalent.

### Primary curriculum areas
Theory of the Montessori method, Developmental Psychology, and practical lectures in: **Exercises of Practical Life (EPL), Sensorial Activities, Language, Arithmetic**, plus Geometry, Botany, Geography, Zoology and Science.

**Curriculum detail blocks** (full text available for course-detail pages):
- *Introduction to Montessori* — life & works of Dr. Maria Montessori, her method & philosophy. Topics: (1) Life & works, method & philosophy; (2) Prepared Environment, Freedom of Choice, Sensitive Periods, Mixed Age Groups, Access & Proportion; (3) Phases of Development; (4) Teacher's role as a Montessori Directress; (5) Presenting lessons the Montessori way; (6) Organizing & managing a Montessori classroom.
- *Exercises of Practical Life (EPL)* — simple daily-life actions that build independence, coordination and grace.
- *Exercises of Sensorial Development* — sharpening the senses via visual, tactile, olfactory, gustatory and hearing exercises.
- *Language activities* — phonics, reading, writing, grammar, spoken language; 44 phonic sounds / 144 alphabetic representations; pink/blue/green level progression; metal insets & sandpaper letters.
- *Arithmetic* — Montessori math materials: counting, quantities & numerals to ten, decimal system with golden beads, addition/subtraction/multiplication/division, linear counting to 1,000.
- *Culture* — botany, zoology, physical sciences, history, geography, arts, dance and music.

### Diploma-level papers
- Paper 1: Phonics & Computers in Montessori Classroom
