# Jolly Kids Montessori — Design Research & Inspiration

> Research to guide the redesign (not a build). Pairs with `content-inventory.md`. Compiled 2026-09-20.
> **Key audience insight:** unlike a Montessori *school* site (audience = parents), this is a Montessori *teacher-training institute* — the primary audience is **adult learners** (housewives, working professionals, graduates aged 18+) deciding to invest in a career. So the design should feel **warm + child-friendly** (Montessori spirit) *but also* **credible, professional, and career-focused** (certification, placement, ROI). Split the difference — approachable, not cartoonish.
>
> **TWO audiences (confirmed by client brochure):** (1) **Aspiring teachers** → courses + free consultation; (2) **Schools/institutions** → B2B "Services to Schools" (workshops, webinars, teacher qualification per NEP 2020, franchise "business model", setting up Montessori environments). The homepage should offer **two clear entry paths**. See `content-inventory.md` §12.
>
> **New trust anchors to feature prominently:** International Curriculum · Central Govt. Nodal Agency Certificate · **World Skill Council (London, UK) Authorised Skill Institute** · AMI & IMTC mentors · **1000+ teachers · 10+ countries · Since 2019**. These are the strongest credibility levers — put a badge strip near the hero.

## 1. What the research says (2026 best practices)

### Conversion & structure (most important for a lead-gen site)
- **CTA above the fold** — "Book a Free Consultation" / "Enquire Now" visible immediately, repeated through the page. (Old site buries it.)
- **Clear "what to expect"** — courses, duration, eligibility, fees, outcomes laid out simply.
- **Real reviews / social proof** high on the page — testimonials + awards build trust for a purchase decision.
- **Functional tools convert:** enquiry form, WhatsApp/call button, tour/consultation booking, downloadable brochure, fee/course info request. Sunshine House-style "tuition calculator" and "tour form" are proven patterns.
- **Reduce word count** — 2026 trend is *less* text, let design and hierarchy carry meaning. The old site is text-heavy; break it into scannable cards, stats, icons.
- **Smaller sitemap, hyper-focused key pages** — Home, Courses, About, Contact do the heavy lifting.

### Look & feel
- **Authentic photography beats stock** — real classroom, materials, students, award photos build far more trust. (Playbook Partner's #1 repeated lesson.) → We must get real photos from the client.
- **Video brings programs to life** — a short hero video/montage of classes, materials, graduation is the single biggest "wow" upgrade (Sentia, Learning Tree, Flamingo Island all lead with video).
- **Cohesive palette + generous whitespace** — LilyPad/North Star win on calm, readable, well-spaced layouts with strong information hierarchy.
- **Custom illustrations / icons + a consistent theme/motif** — Minnows (hand-drawn nature), Goslings (duckling motif), Learning Tree (leaf motif). A subtle recurring motif (e.g., Montessori materials — the pink tower, geometric shapes, wooden textures) can give Jolly Kids a distinct identity.
- **Bold, expressive, brand-led design** — 2026 rejects generic templates. This is the biggest opportunity vs. the old cookie-cutter WordPress theme.
- **Mobile-first** — most Indian users will visit on a phone; design for that first.
- **Accessibility** — strong contrast, readable type, keyboard/screen-reader friendly.

## 2. Recommended visual direction

### Color palette (warm-professional, Montessori-inspired)
Amber works best as a **5–15% accent** against deep, calm neutrals. Proposed tokens:

| Role | Suggestion | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / trust | **Deep navy** `#1B2A4A` (or teal-navy) | Headings, header, credibility |
| Accent / CTA | **Amber/marigold** `#F4A215` (from old brand) | Buttons, highlights, only where action matters |
| Warm secondary | Terracotta / coral `#E8734A` *(optional)* | Playful warmth, Montessori material tones |
| Background | **Cream / warm off-white** `#FBF7F0` | Soft, welcoming, high readability |
| Support neutral | Charcoal `#2B2B2B`, soft grey `#6B7280` | Body text, captions |
| Success/nature | Muted sage green `#7BA05B` *(optional)* | Nature-based Montessori nod, badges |

*Montessori materials themselves (pink tower, red rods, blue triangles, golden beads, wooden browns) are a great source for an authentic, ownable secondary palette.*

### Typography
- **Headings:** a friendly-but-confident geometric/rounded sans — e.g. **Poppins**, **Fredoka**, **Quicksand**, or **Nunito** (rounded = approachable, still professional).
- **Body:** a clean, highly readable sans — **Inter**, **Open Sans**, or **Nunito Sans**.
- Pairing rule: one rounded display for warmth + one neutral workhorse for readability. Keep it to 2 families.

### Motifs & imagery
- Recurring motif: simple line icons of Montessori materials, rounded shapes, soft blobs, gentle wood/paper textures.
- Rounded corners, soft shadows, generous padding — feels safe and modern.
- Real photos in organic/rounded frames; avoid stiff rectangular stock grids.

**Home**
1. Hero — headline + subhead + "Book Free Consultation" CTA + hero image/video; trust badges (Govt. Certified · 100% Placement · Since 2019).

8. Awards & recognitions (logos/badges strip).

**Testimonials** — full wall + "share yours" form (fix the "accounting" copy bug).

**Contact** — form + Google Map + phone/WhatsApp/email + hours.

## 5. Differentiators to lean into (Jolly Kids' unique angles)
- **Career outcomes** — Central Govt. certificate, valid abroad, 100% placement assistance, "start your own school." Sell the *career/ROI*, not just the philosophy.
- **Global reach** — students & trainers across UAE, Singapore, New Zealand, Dubai, Oman, Qatar. Show a world map / "trained teachers in 7+ countries."
- **Founder credibility** — Dr. Joshla's story + 7 awards (2022–2024, incl. Dubai). Strong personal-brand trust anchor.
- **Accessibility of entry** — for housewives & working professionals; Tamil + English medium; flexible fees. Very inclusive, emotionally resonant messaging.
- **Hands-on rigor** — 350+ Montessori materials, demos, viva, spoken English & interview training.
