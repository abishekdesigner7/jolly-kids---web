# Jolly Kids Montessori — Inspiration Board (from Behance)

> Curated from Behance, browsed 2026-09-20 (search: "kids education website ui" / "montessori website"). Complements `inspiration-mobbin.md` (which covers modern site *section patterns*). Behance adds **full brand-identity + visual-language** direction: color, character/mascot systems, illustration, and how education brands feel end-to-end.
>
> **Filter note:** Behance education results skew toward *preschools* (playful, kid/parent audience). Jolly Kids trains *adults*, so take the **warmth, color confidence, and craft** from these — but dial the playfulness down toward professional/premium (blend with the BoxApps example below).

---

## ⭐ 1. Precious Angels — Preschool Brand Identity & Website (top pick)
*Award-winning — Featured on World Brand Design Society. 16K+ views.*

**What makes it great**
- **Custom geometric mascot system** — a family of simple rounded shapes (blue/green/red/orange/purple) with little eyes. Distinct, ownable, scalable across the brand.
- **Bold black display typography** ("PRECIOUS ANGELS PRESCHOOL") — confident, not babyish. The playfulness comes from color + shapes, not the type. This is the key lesson.
- **Clear single CTA** — mobile hero reads *"Welcome to Precious Angels Preschool — Science based, hands-on learning — BOOK A TOUR."*
- **Confident color blocking** — full-bleed orange/purple panels behind photos and phone mockups.
- Real kid/classroom photography combined with the flat shape system.

**Steal for Jolly Kids**
- A **custom shape/mascot mark** inspired by *actual Montessori materials* (pink tower, geometric solids, knobbed cylinders) — an ownable visual signature instead of generic clip-art.
- The formula **bold neutral display type + confident color accents** = warm yet credible (ideal for an adult audience).
- CTA parallel: their *"Book a Tour"* → your *"Book a Free Consultation."* Their *"Science based, hands-on learning"* → your *"Scientifically proven, 100+ year Montessori method."*
- Color-blocked full-bleed section bands to structure a long page.

## 2. BoxApps — Educational Platform for Schoolchildren (Lena Kovalenko)
*Clean e-learning/LMS product design (for a Ministry of Education).*

**What makes it great**
- **Professional, calm light-blue palette** — proof that "education" doesn't have to mean loud primary colors. Feels trustworthy and grown-up.
- **"Photo + icon-benefit list" section** — a real photo on the left, and 4 benefits each with a small brand-glyph icon on the right. Extremely reusable.
- Tidy **auth/forms** and consistent iconography built from the logo mark.

**Steal for Jolly Kids**
- The **photo + 4-icon-benefit** layout → perfect for "Why Choose Us" (Expert Faculty / Holistic Approach / Practical Experience / Community) or the founder's specialities.
- A **calm, professional base palette** (navy + cream + one accent) as the backbone, with warm accents layered on — this is the "adult, credible" half of the blend.

## 3. Broader Behance education trend (across results)
Recurring signals in the strongest kids/education projects:
- **Rounded everything** — soft corners, pill buttons, blob/rounded-rectangle shapes.
- **Bold friendly sans display type** (heavy weights) + clean readable body.
- **Bright but *controlled* palette** — 1 dominant + 2–3 accents, lots of white/cream space (not rainbow chaos).
- **Custom illustration/shape systems + real photography together** (not stock-only).
- **Phone mockups in the hero** showing the primary CTA — signals mobile-first.
- **One clear primary action** repeated (Book a Tour / Enroll / Get Started).

---

## 4. The Jolly Kids blend (synthesis of both boards)
Combine the two references into one direction:

> **BoxApps professionalism + Precious Angels warmth & craft — minus the childishness.**

- **Base:** navy + warm cream, generous whitespace, clean readable type (credible, adult).
- **Warmth:** amber/marigold accent (matches WSC certificate), soft rounded shapes, an ownable Montessori-materials-inspired shape/mascot motif.
- **Proof:** bold display headlines + inline stat strips (Mobbin: Titan/Norma) + accreditation logo strip (Mobbin: ClickUp) → WSC London, Central Govt, AMI, IMTC.
- **Structure:** color-blocked full-bleed section bands (Precious Angels) to organize a long, content-rich page.
- **Photo + icon-benefit** blocks (BoxApps) for value props and specialities.
- **One clear CTA** everywhere: *Book a Free Consultation.*
- **Two audience paths:** For Aspiring Teachers / For Schools.
