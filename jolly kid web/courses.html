Primary curriculum

What you'll learn

The curriculum covers Montessori theory, developmental psychology and hands-on practical demonstration across five core areas and beyond.

Understand the Montessori system and its uniqueness — the life and works of Dr. Maria Montessori, her philosophy and theories. Topics include the prepared environment, freedom of choice, sensitive periods, mixed-age groups, phases of development, the teacher's role as a Montessori directress, how to present lessons the Montessori way, and how to organise and manage a Montessori classroom.
Simple daily-life actions — from pouring water to washing dishes — introduced at the right age so children learn to do things themselves. These build independence, concentration, coordination and grace of movement, self-respect and a love for work and nature that lasts a lifetime.
The senses are bridges to the environment. Through carefully designed materials and highly capturing visuals, children sharpen their visual, tactile, olfactory, gustatory and auditory senses — the foundation for all later learning.
A multi-dimensional approach covering phonics, reading, writing, grammar and spoken language. Children learn 44 phonic sounds with 144 alphabetic representations, progressing through pink, blue and green levels to total reading — with metal insets and sandpaper letters preparing the hand for writing.
Montessori's remarkable maths materials teach counting, quantities and numerals through concrete experience. From the decimal system with golden beads to addition, subtraction, multiplication and division, and linear counting to one thousand — abstract concepts become tangible and joyful.
Life sciences (botany & zoology), physical sciences, history, geography, arts, dance and music. Children explore the whole world — continents, countries, people, cultures, terrain and natural phenomena — developing personality and independence through concrete materials.

Terms & conditions

A good teacher knows & follows the rules

What we expect from every trainee — and what you can expect from us.

  1. Dress decently and neatly for all classes, and wear the uniform saree whenever instructed by Jolly Kids Montessori Institute.
  2. One to two compulsory theory & practical classes are planned each week; class timings are shared regularly.
  3. Follow the rules and regulations of the management.
  4. We insist on strict discipline and give personal attention. Classes are handled by experienced AMI & IMTC-trained Montessori adults, professors and lecturers.
  5. Be in class 10 minutes before the training session begins.
  6. The management may dismiss any trainee found misbehaving, misleading others or spreading negative comments.
  7. The management has full authority to admit or refuse admission without giving a reason.
  8. Take prior permission before taking leave.
  9. Fees once paid will not be refunded or adjusted for a future session or year.
  10. Teaching practice, demo classes and the viva exam are compulsory.
